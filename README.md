# RETAIL-SALES-ANALYSIS

Retail Sales Forecasting & Customer Purchase Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project is developed as part of a Data Science Internship – Minor Project.

The objective is to analyze historical retail sales data, understand customer purchasing behavior, identify sales trends, forecast future sales, and provide useful business recommendations through Python and Power BI.

🎯 Business Problem

A national retail chain is experiencing fluctuations in sales across different product categories and store locations.

Management wants to understand:

Which products and categories perform best?
Which stores generate the highest revenue?
How does sales change over time?
What customer purchasing patterns can be identified?
What will sales look like over the next three months?
How can inventory, staffing, and promotions be improved?
🎯 Project Objectives
Collect and clean retail sales data.
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
Identify sales and customer purchasing patterns.
Create useful features from transaction data.
Build a machine learning model for sales forecasting.
Forecast sales for the next three months.
Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.
Provide data-driven business recommendations.

📐 Important Power BI Measures
Total Revenue
Total Revenue =
SUMX(
    retail_sales,
    retail_sales[Sales_Amount] *
    (1 - retail_sales[Discount_Percent] / 100)
)
Total Sales
Total Sales =
SUM(retail_sales[Sales_Amount])
Total Profit
Total Profit =
SUM(retail_sales[Profit])
Total Quantity
Total Quantity =
SUM(retail_sales[Quantity])
Total Transactions
Total Transactions =
DISTINCTCOUNT(retail_sales[Transaction_ID])
Average Order Value
Average Order Value =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Revenue],
    [Total Transactions]
)
Total Forecast Sales
Total Forecast Sales =
SUM(forecast_results[Forecast_Sales])
Average Forecast Sales
Average Forecast Sales =
AVERAGE(forecast_results[Forecast_Sales])
